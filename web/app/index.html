    Using a Jellyfin server

    As the folder above (recommended). Your music folder can be the shared folder of a Jellyfin server, such as a mapped network drive. This program then works on the files directly, so ripping, fixing tags, covers and lyrics all work, and Jellyfin (with the TVs and phones that play from it) shows the changes after it rescans. This program can ask it to rescan, and shares your flags and playlists with it; both are set on the Jellyfin tab.

    As the server alone, with no folder on this computer. The songs, covers and lyrics then come from the server, and this program plays and casts them, with favorites, playlists and flags. Jellyfin does not let other programs change its files, so ripping to it and editing tags, covers or lyrics is not possible in this mode: the Rip a CD page and most of Review are hidden, and the TVs you pair here still use the folder (they can use the server themselves).

    Drive checks and the MP3 quality are on the Rip a CD page.

    Cast to a TV or speaker

    Use the TV button in the player bar to play your queue on a TV or speaker; the button then becomes its remote, and playing on this computer takes the music back. Share the library with TVs first, under TV.

    TVs with the MediaVerse Studio app: open the app on the TV and choose this computer. The TV plays the queue with its own Now Playing screen (album wall, lyrics).

    Google Cast devices play through Google's own player for now (cover, title and progress). The MediaVerse look on Chromecasts and Google TVs (album wall, lyrics) is still to come.
