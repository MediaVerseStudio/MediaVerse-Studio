The GPL covers the code, not the project's identity. The name **"MediaVerse Studio"**, the MediaVerse Studio logo
and the mediaverse.studio domain belong to the project's author and are not licensed under the GPL.

- **Unchanged copies:** you may share unchanged copies of official MediaVerse Studio releases under their own
  name and logo.
- **Forks and changed versions:** if you share a version you have changed, give it its own name and logo. Do
  not present it as MediaVerse Studio or as an official release. You may say that it is "based on MediaVerse
  Studio" and link to the original project.
- **Talking about the project:** articles, reviews, tutorials, packages that point to official releases, and
  links to the project are welcome and need no permission.
- **Anything else:** for other uses of the name or logo, ask first (contact@mediaverse.studio).

This keeps people from mistaking someone else's build for the real thing. It does not limit what the GPL allows
you to do with the code.
